🛡️ Insurance Risk & Claim Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)
📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze insurance policies, claim patterns, and risk factors. It helps insurers understand claim behavior, customer segmentation, and financial risk exposure for better decision-making.

🚀 Key Metrics
📄 Total Policies: 37,542
💰 Total Claim Amount: $187.8M
📊 Average Claim Frequency: 0.5
💵 Average Claim Amount: $5K
📊 Dashboard Features
1️⃣ Claim Analysis

Claim amount by Car Use (Private vs Commercial)
Claim distribution by Car Make
Coverage zone analysis (Urban, Rural, etc.)
Claim trends over years
2️⃣ Policy Distribution

Total policies segmented by:
Car usage
Car brand
Customer age groups
Policy growth trend over time

3️⃣ Customer Demographics
👨 Male: 18.7K
👩 Female: 18.8K
Balanced gender distribution
Age group insights:
Highest claims from 26–65 age groups
Lower activity in younger & older segments

4️⃣ Risk Factors Analysis
🚗 Claims by Kids Driving (0–3)
🎓 Claims by Education Level
💍 Combined analysis:
Education vs Marital Status

👉 Example Insight:
Married customers with bachelor degrees contribute highest claim value

5️⃣ Advanced Insights
📈 Claim trend peaked post-2000 then stabilized
🚙 Private car usage dominates total claims
🌍 Claims are evenly distributed across coverage zones
🎯 Certain car brands (like Ford, Chevrolet) contribute highest claim amounts

🛠 Tools & Technologies
Power BI Desktop
Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
DAX (KPIs & Measures)
Data Modeling & Relationships

📈 Key Insights
Private vehicle users contribute majority of claims
Claim amount is highly concentrated among a few car brands
Middle-age group customers are the highest risk segment
Education & marital status significantly influence claim patterns
