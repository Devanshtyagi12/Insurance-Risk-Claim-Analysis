# Insurance Risk & Claim Analysis — Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze insurance policy data, identify risk patterns, and understand claim behavior across customer demographics and vehicle types.

## Numbers at a Glance
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Policies Analyzed | 37,542 |
| Total Claim Amount | $187.8M |
| Average Claim Frequency | 0.5 |
| Average Claim Amount | $5K |
| Gender Split | Male 18.7K / Female 18.8K |

## What This Dashboard Covers
**Claim Analysis** — Claim amount by car use (Private vs Commercial), distribution by car brand, coverage zone breakdown (Urban / Rural), claim trends over time

**Policy Distribution** — Segmented by car usage, brand, and customer age group. Policy growth trend over time

**Customer Demographics** — Age group analysis: highest claims from 26–65 segment. Gender distribution nearly equal

**Risk Factor Analysis** — Claims by number of kids driving (0–3), education level, and combined Education × Marital Status breakdown

**Key Insight** — Married customers with bachelor's degrees contribute the highest total claim value

## Key Findings
- Private vehicle users account for the majority of claims
- Claim amounts are concentrated among a few car brands (Ford, Chevrolet top the list)
- Middle-age group (26–65) is the highest-risk segment
- Education and marital status are meaningful predictors of claim patterns
